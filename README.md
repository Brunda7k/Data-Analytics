# 🎬 Project 1: Netflix Data Analysis

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes Netflix content data to uncover trends in movies and TV shows. The analysis explores content distribution, growth over time, genres, and country-wise contributions.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Compare Movies vs TV Shows distribution
- Analyze content added over the years
- Identify top contributing countries
- Explore popular genres
- Study trends in ratings and duration

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## 📈 Key Insights
- Movies make up a larger share of content than TV Shows.
- Content additions increased significantly after 2015.
- The United States and India are major contributors.
- Drama and International Movies dominate the platform.

# 🛒 Project 2: Superstore Sales Analysis

## 📌 Overview
This project performs detailed sales analysis on the Superstore dataset to extract meaningful business insights. It covers product performance, regional sales distribution, customer segments, and time-based trends.

## 📂 Dataset Details
- 9,789 records
- 18 columns
- Includes Order Date, Region, Category, Segment, Product Name, and Sales

## 🎯 Objectives
- Clean and prepare sales data
- Identify top-selling products
- Analyze sales by region and category
- Study customer segment contributions
- Visualize monthly sales trends

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### 🔹 Top 10 Products by Sales
Identified highest revenue-generating products using aggregation and visualized with bar charts.

### 🔹 Sales by Region
Compared regional performance to identify strong and weak markets.

### 🔹 Sales by Category
Analyzed which product categories drive the most revenue.

### 🔹 Monthly Sales Trend
Performed time-series analysis to detect patterns and seasonal fluctuations.

### 🔹 Sales by Customer Segment
Examined contribution of Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.

## 📈 Key Insights
- A small number of products generate a large portion of total sales.
- Certain regions outperform others consistently.
- Category-wise sales show clear revenue leaders.
- Monthly trends reveal growth patterns.
- Consumer segment contributes the highest share of sales.
